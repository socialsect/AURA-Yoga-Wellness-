# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Next.js (App Router, static export) + Three.js + GSAP/ScrollTrigger + Lenis. Same stack as the studio's previous concept (Aurelia), so it deploys the same way (GitHub Pages).

## Users

Primary: the client (a studio / agency principal) reviewing a portfolio concept from Harshita, after the Aurelia longevity concept. Secondary, in-world: adults looking for a yoga teacher. Some are local to Puducherry and want dawn classes, some are abroad and want live online classes or a retreat. They are deciding whether this teacher is right for them and what to book.

## Product Purpose

A concept website for a single yoga instructor (not a studio chain). It has to feel calm, premium and immersive, carry 3D animation and interaction throughout, and make booking a class obvious. Success means the client is impressed on first view, and an in-world visitor understands who the teacher is, what she teaches, when and where, what it costs, and how to book.

## Positioning

The client asked for something that does not look like a typical yoga website: no beige-and-sage template, no lotus icon, no stock beach pose with "find your inner peace". The previous concept (Aurelia) set the bar: a cinematic scroll story and smooth UX.

## Operating Context

Concept brand. The instructor is fictional, and every name, schedule, price and bio is illustrative sample content. It must be labelled as a concept, as Aurelia was.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Client brief: fluid 3D movement, human forms, abstract shapes and nature-inspired elements, used throughout the site, not only in the hero.
- Must stay fast and usable: smooth scroll, reduced-motion fallback, works on phones.
- No booking backend: actions open a pre-filled email.

## Evidence on Hand

None real. No testimonials, press, student counts or certifications exist, and none may be invented. Photography comes from openly licensed sources, is credited, and is never presented as the instructor herself.

## Product Principles

1. The experience of practice comes before information about practice, but information is never hidden.
2. Every 3D moment carries meaning (breath, pose, light), never decoration.
3. Calm does not mean empty: full content, real schedule, real prices (sample).
4. Clear, honest booking path from every screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard access, a `prefers-reduced-motion` path that keeps all content, and readable type on phones.
