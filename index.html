How Ahana teaches

I teach yoga for people who want to feel calmer, stronger and more at home in their own bodies, one slow breath at a time.

Meet Ahana

Ahana means the first light of the sun. She has taught yoga since 2012: slow, clear classes built on the breath rather than on how a pose looks.

She trained in hatha and vinyasa in Mysore and Rishikesh, then in yin and restorative yoga. Most students come to her stiff, tired or anxious. They stay because they leave each class feeling more like themselves.
