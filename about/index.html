Meet Ahana

Yoga teacher since 2012. Hatha, vinyasa and yin, in English and Tamil.

Ahana means the first light of the sun.

She has taught yoga since 2012: slow, clear classes built on the breath rather than on how a pose looks.

Most students come to her stiff, tired or anxious. They stay because they leave each class feeling more like themselves. Classes are in English and Tamil, in the studio, at your home, online and on a yearly retreat.

How she teaches

Breath before shape

A pose counts when the breath stays long inside it. If the breath turns short, we come back a step.

Slow enough to notice

We hold, rest and repeat. You learn what your knees, hips and shoulders are telling you today.

Eight mats at most

Small rooms, always. Every cue is meant for someone in the room, and you can ask for help at any point.

Where it comes from

  • Hatha

    Trained in Mysore and Rishikesh

    The base of every morning class: steady standing poses, long breathing, a full rest.

  • Vinyasa

    Trained in Mysore and Rishikesh

    Slow, linked movement, one breath to one step, for strength without hurry.

  • Yin and restorative

    Further training

    Quiet floor poses held with cushions and blankets, for tired and anxious weeks.
