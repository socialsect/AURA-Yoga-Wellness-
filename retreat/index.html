Seven mornings by the water

8 to 15 February 2027. Twelve guests at a lagoon in the Western Ghats.

Sunrise practice, long silences, early nights

Two classes a day, swims and simple vegetarian food.

Reserve a place

One day there

  1. 6:00 am

    First light practice

    Breathing and a slow flow while the mist is still on the water.

  2. 8:30 am

    Breakfast together

    Warm porridge, fruit and tea, with no hurry to leave the table.

  3. 11:00 am

    Swim or silence

    The pool, a book or a long quiet hour. Nothing is compulsory.

  4. 5:00 pm

    Evening practice

    Floor poses held with cushions as the heat leaves the day.

  5. 7:30 pm

    Supper and an early night

    Simple vegetarian food, then lamps low and an early bed.

Each day has a word

Monday 15 February: breakfast, goodbyes and your ride to the airport.
